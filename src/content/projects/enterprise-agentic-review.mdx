## Problem & Operational Bottleneck

Operations reviewers previously cross-checked three disconnected internal platforms alongside eight Amazon Redshift scripts refreshed only twice daily. Gathering context for a single complex customer review consumed repetitive lookup time and forced decisions on stale snapshots.

## Architecture & Engineering Trade-offs

Rather than replacing governed enterprise tooling with an ungoverned standalone web app, I combined **UiPath Studio Web**, **Maestro orchestration**, two core RPA/API extraction processes, and a **validated LLM summarization agent**:

* **Live aggregation over batch refreshes:** Data is pulled on demand when an operator opens a case, replacing two scheduled daily batch cycles.
* **Deterministic pre-aggregation:** Structured SQL metrics from Redshift are calculated deterministically before reaching the LLM, preventing arithmetic hallucinations.
* **Enterprise PII boundary:** Sensitive customer attributes remain strictly inside the enterprise perimeter; outputs pass schema validation before rendering.
* **Human-in-the-Loop ownership:** The AI agent accelerates comprehension and highlights structured anomalies, while final operational decisions stay 100% with the reviewer.

## Measured Outcome

* Reduced operator data-review time by **approximately 70%** across target review workflows.
* Eliminated multi-tool context switching while maintaining full auditability and zero external PII egress.
* Part of **40+ end-to-end production automation workflows** delivered from discovery through hypercare at Betfair Romania Development.
