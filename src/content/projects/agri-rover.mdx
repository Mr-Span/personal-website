Completed as my BSc Robotics thesis at the Technical University of Cluj-Napoca (UTCN), **Agri-Rover** integrates custom mechanical assembly, electrical wiring, and closed-loop embedded C++ firmware for multi-waypoint orchard navigation, complementary heading stabilization, environmental telemetry streaming, and Bluetooth failsafe motor stop.
